## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a collectible sticker emblem, not a realistic miniature. Thick contour with handmade error, strong silhouette, flat spot color and a little risograph or screen-print texture. Compact and complete, like a die-cut badge. A star, a dot or a plant may serve the subject. Cream, warm yellow, pink, coral, vintage blue, plant green or deep brown-black. Reject realistic proportion, a complex scene, smooth vector and a corporate lockup.
